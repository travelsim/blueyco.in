2026 Bluey Impressions Release 1: prices and cover quantities

Release 1 of the Australia Post 2026 Impressions range went on sale online only at 8:30am AEDT on 23 November 2026. Release 2 follows at 8:30am AEDT on 26 November 2026. Orders are at auspost.com.au/impressions.

How to read the numbers. A PNC (postal numismatic cover) mounts coins on a stamped cover with a postmark. A minisheet collection pairs coins with a Bluey minisheet in a presentation folder and is not a PNC. The boxed set of 5 holds five themed minisheet collections in one box. Every quantity below is the number of covers produced by Australia Post — never a coin mintage. The coins are struck by the Royal Australian Mint (RAM); cover production is an Australia Post figure. Covers are 190x110 format with a gold star MyStamp.

Collector questions

Are the published quantities coin mintages?
No. The figures in the table are the number of covers produced by Australia Post, not coin mintages. The coins are struck by the Royal Australian Mint; cover production is an Australia Post figure.
What is the difference between a PNC, a minisheet collection and the boxed set?
A PNC is a postal numismatic cover: coins mounted on a stamped cover with a postmark. A minisheet collection pairs coins with a Bluey minisheet in a presentation folder. The boxed set of 5 holds five themed minisheet collections in one box.
