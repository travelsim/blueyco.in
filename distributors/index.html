Authorised Royal Australian Mint distributors

The Royal Australian Mint accredits Australian retailers to sell its collectables. After a Bluey ballot closes, remaining stock is released through the Mint Shop, the Mint Contact Centre (1300 652 020) and participating distributors — not every distributor receives every release, so check stock with them first. See also the ballot guide.

Why buy from an authorised distributor? Genuine Mint product at Mint-set pricing rather than inflated re-seller asking prices.
“Investment” distributors sell bullion; for Bluey coins look for those marked Numismatic.
