# Secondary Market Valuations & Global Demand Evidence (2026)

Documented research notes on secondary market pricing premiums and mainstream streaming audience metrics supporting sustained numismatic demand.

## 1. Valuation & Secondary Market Multiples
- **Source:** *The Conversation* / *Businesses.com.au* syndication (published 18 May 2026; author: silver-price researcher).
- **Canonical URLs:**
  - `https://theconversation.com/why-bluey-coins-are-worth-big-dollarbucks-far-more-than-the-silver-theyre-made-from-282275`
  - `https://agency.businesses.com.au/australian-news/the-conversation/100756-why-bluey-coins-are-worth-big-%E2%80%98dollarbucks%E2%80%99-%E2%80%93-far-more-than-the-silver-they%E2%80%99re-made-from`
  - Wayback capture: `https://web.archive.org/web/20260522032330/https://theconversation.com/why-bluey-coins-are-worth-big-dollarbucks-far-more-than-the-silver-theyre-made-from-282275`
- **Key Findings:**
  - Official A$2 Bluey collectable dollar releases from the Royal Australian Mint (RAM) have experienced extreme secondary demand.
  - The 99.9% pure silver Heeler family proof coin originally retailed at A$115 and resells on secondary markets at up to 6× issue price (~A$690).
  - Preceding silver releases (September 2024 and October 2024) experienced immediate sellouts.
  - High public demand caused the Australia Post release site to crash within minutes on launch day, prompting allocation ballots via EQL.
  - RAM mintage breakdown: across two years of issues ($2 till coins, tube/folder sets, and coloured AlBr coins), only three strictly limited 99.9% silver proof editions were struck:
    - 2024 Bluey Dollarbucks: 2,650 coins.
    - 2025 Christmas Bauble: 10,000 coins.
    - 2026 Heeler Family: 12,000 coins.

## 2. Mainstream US Audience & Streaming Scale
- **Source:** *ABC News* (published 29 January 2026; by Liana Walker; reporting Nielsen ARTEY streaming data).
- **Canonical URL:** `https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-01-29/bluey-tops-streaming-charts-in-us-second-year-running/106284782`
- **Key Findings:**
  - *Bluey* ranked as the #1 most-streamed title in the United States for the second consecutive year (2024 and 2025).
  - Total US viewing time reached **45.2 billion minutes** in 2025.
  - Outperformed major adult evergreen and franchise titles in the US, including *Grey's Anatomy* (40.9B minutes, #2) and *Stranger Things* Season 5 (40.0B minutes, #3).
  - Confirms a large, active international fan base outside Australia, creating strong cross-border collector appetite for authentic Australian Mint and Australia Post merchandise.
