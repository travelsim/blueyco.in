## Correction 2026-10-07: only 10 PNC designs are retail products

Australia Post Stamp Bulletin 391 (collectables.auspost.com.au, 2024) lists exactly ten Bluey PNCs at $25.95 (items 2278401-2278410) plus a $260 ten-PNC set limited to 1,000 sets. The annual-report rows 11635, 11637, 11638, 11640, 11641 and 11645 have no retail listing, price, image or sold evidence, so the six `s1_pnc_116xx` records were removed rather than shown as separate products. Their codes may be channel or manufacturing line items for the same covers; unresolved. Row 11645 (150) coincides with the Impressions silver proof prestige PNC (150) and row 11624 (5,000) with `s1_proof_coloured` (5,000); 11624 is now recorded as that coin's product code, inferred from the matching quantity.

## Incident evidence (sourced, stored separately from product records)

Annual Performance Statement, printed p. 36 — two distinct events:

1. One notifiable incident during the financial year, occurring outside normal hours
   and off-site. ComCare was notified per the Mint's obligations and declined to
   take the matter further.
2. One security breach: on 3 July 2024 the Mint's security team received reports of
   limited-edition Bluey coins sold on eBay before public release. Internal
   investigation plus external investigations with the dispatch provider and
   relevant authorities; no evidence of internal fraud. Authorities arrested and
   charged three members of the public and recovered/returned 39,641 coins.

Work health & safety, printed p. 29: 99 health and safety incidents in 2024–25
(Figure 1 monthly breakdown); one notifiable incident reported to Comcare; lost
time injury frequency rate 4.42 vs industry benchmark 12.7.
